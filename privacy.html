Privacy Policy

Critique Cinema for iPhone · Effective November 2, 2026

The short version

Critique Cinema does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your reviews live on your iPhone. The developer never receives your reviews, notes, searches or shared links.

What stays on your iPhone

Your reviews, scores, notes, Watch Soon list, posters and settings are saved only on your device. If you use iCloud Backup, Apple includes them in your phone's backup under Apple's own privacy policy. We never see them.

Searching for titles

When you search for a movie or show, the words you type are sent to public title databases to find matches: Apple's iTunes Search API, TVMaze, and a community IMDb-style search service. Poster images are downloaded from the addresses those services return. These requests contain only your search text, not your name or reviews. Those services may record ordinary web request details such as your IP address, under their own policies.

Backups you export

If you use Export, the app creates a file with your archive. Where that file goes (Files, iCloud Drive, a message) is entirely your choice.

Sharing a review

When you share a review, the app makes a picture of it and a link. The link contains the title's details and the review you chose to include: scores, and, only if you leave those options on, your notes and the name you typed. That information sits inside the link itself and is sent through whichever messaging or sharing app you choose. It is not uploaded to us and we do not store it, but anyone who receives or is forwarded the link can read it. When someone opens a link and does not have the app, this website shows an "App required" page; the review part of the link (after the "#") is never sent to the website. Website visits are handled by GitHub Pages, which may record ordinary request details such as IP addresses under GitHub's privacy policy.

Reviews shared with you

If you open a review someone shared, it is shown to you on your device. It is not saved unless you choose "Add to Watch Soon", which saves only the title's details (not their scores, notes or name).

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions? Open a support request.
